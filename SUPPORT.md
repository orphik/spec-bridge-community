# Customer Support & SLA Policy — Spec Bridge

**Business Hours:** Monday – Friday, 09:00 – 18:00 US Eastern Time (excluding major US holidays)

---

## Support Channels

1. **Private Enterprise Support Desk (24h SLA):**
   - **Customer Portal:** https://orphik.atlassian.net/helpcenter/SUP/contact-us *(or use the **Priority Support Portal** button inside Spec Bridge's in-app `💬 Support & Feature Feedback` drawer)*
   - Best for private troubleshooting, workflow/hierarchy setup, billing questions, or enterprise security inquiries.
2. **Community Feature Roadmap & Q&A:**
   - [**GitHub Discussions**](https://github.com/orphik/spec-bridge-community/discussions) — propose enhancements, upvote roadmap items, and share IDE agent workflows.
   - [**GitHub Issues**](https://github.com/orphik/spec-bridge-community/issues/new/choose) — submit public enhancement requests or reproducible UI bug reports.

---

## Target Initial Response SLAs

| Severity | Definition | Target Initial Response |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Severity 1 (Critical)** | Spec Bridge unavailable in production across work items, or workflow gatekeeper blocking valid transitions with no workaround. | **Within 4 business hours** |
| **Severity 2 (High)** | Core capability degraded (e.g., PRD Drift check, Acceptance Criteria cascade, or MCP pull failing) with partial workaround. | **Within 1 business day (8 business hours)** |
| **Severity 3 (Standard)** | UI polish, hierarchy/workflow configuration guidance, or enhancement request. | **Within 2 business days** |

---

## Fast-Track Troubleshooting Tip

Before opening a support ticket, click **`💬 Support & Feature Feedback` → `📋 Copy Diagnostic Snapshot (Zero PII)`** inside any Jira issue running Spec Bridge and paste that JSON block into your ticket—it includes your app build, Jira hierarchy level, and environment status with **zero PRD or customer text**.
